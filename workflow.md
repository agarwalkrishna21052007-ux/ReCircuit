# Operational Workflow

1. Place discarded PCB in scanning area.
2. Capture PCB images.
3. Detect and classify candidate components.
4. Determine target coordinates.
5. Select recovery targets.
6. Move extraction mechanism to target.
7. Perform controlled extraction.
8. Transfer component to testing area.
9. Run appropriate electrical test.
10. Classify component.
11. Assign reuse grade.
12. Store result in digital inventory.
